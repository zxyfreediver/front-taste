# Mono Ink Style

Use this style when the product should feel serious, typographic, decisive, and print-like.

## Core Principles

- Use black, white, and a small range of grays only.
- Make typography, spacing, and rules do the visual work.
- Prefer strong editorial hierarchy over icons, color, or decoration.
- Treat every panel as a printed brief, ledger, clipping, or operating memo.

## Layout Rules

- Use large headlines, column grids, thick horizontal rules, side notes, and ledger tables.
- Use hard black borders and high contrast.
- In dashboards, make metrics feel like newspaper financial tables.
- In settings, use stark form rows, check marks, rule-separated groups, and clear black actions.

## Component Rules

- Buttons are rectangular black or white slabs with strong borders.
- Badges are small mono labels, not colorful pills.
- Cards should be editorial blocks with rules, not rounded SaaS cards.
- Charts should be simple bars, tick marks, and numeric tables.

## Motion Rules

- Keep motion minimal and purposeful.
- State changes should feel like marking ink on paper.

## Output Checklist

- No colorful accent is required to understand the UI.
- The page can be distinguished by typography and rules alone.
- Every key action remains obvious in black and white.
