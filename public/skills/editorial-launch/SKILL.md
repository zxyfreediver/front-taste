# Editorial Launch Style

Use this style when the product needs storytelling, strong written hierarchy, and a crafted launch-page rhythm.

## Core Principles

- Treat typography as the primary visual asset.
- Use warm paper tones, ink-like text, and restrained clay accents.
- Prefer narrative sections, pull quotes, side notes, and article rhythm.
- Avoid generic feature-card grids and empty marketing claims.

## Layout Rules

- Use large editorial headings and readable body text.
- Mix full-width narrative bands with narrow reading columns.
- Use product UI fragments as evidence, not decoration.

## Component Rules

- Buttons should feel quiet and print-adjacent.
- Cards should be sparse and content-led.
- Changelogs and docs should have excellent spacing and hierarchy.

## Motion Rules

- Use quiet section reveals.
- Avoid playful bounces or busy microinteractions.

## Output Checklist

- The page reads like a launch story.
- Typography has a clear editorial hierarchy.
- Product details support the narrative.
