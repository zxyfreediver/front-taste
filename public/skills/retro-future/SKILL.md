# Retro Future Style

Use this style when the product should feel expressive, energetic, and retro-futuristic while remaining readable.

## Core Principles

- Use a purple night base with neon pink and cyan signal accents.
- Bring in pixel grids, arcade panels, and crisp product states.
- Prefer character and contrast over generic Web3 gloss.
- Avoid unreadable neon text, excessive glow, and cliché cyber clutter.

## Layout Rules

- Use grid-based panels with deliberate rhythm.
- Mix expressive hero areas with clean product UI.
- Keep mobile views simpler and more readable.

## Component Rules

- Buttons can use neon fills, outlines, or terminal-like states.
- Cards should feel like arcade/system panels.
- Forms and settings need practical contrast.

## Motion Rules

- Use scanline-inspired transitions and crisp hover feedback.
- Avoid animation that harms readability.

## Output Checklist

- The UI is memorable but usable.
- Neon accents do not destroy contrast.
- Product surfaces still feel real.
