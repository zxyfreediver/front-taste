# Liquid Glass Style

Use this style when the product should feel like a lightweight OS-native AI layer with translucent depth and calm polish.

## Core Principles

- Use translucent white surfaces over soft color fields.
- Make hierarchy through depth, blur, floating layers, and gentle shadows.
- Prefer capsule controls, rounded panes, and soft highlights.
- Keep content crisp over every glass layer.

## Layout Rules

- Use floating sheets, frosted side panels, OS-like docks, and layered assistant cards.
- In dashboards, use stacked glass metrics and blur-backed activity streams.
- In settings, use capsule toggles, translucent control groups, and calm segmented sections.
- Avoid hard black borders, noisy gradients, and unreadable low-contrast glass.

## Component Rules

- Buttons are rounded capsules with subtle fill or translucent outline.
- Cards should have backdrop blur, soft borders, and visible depth.
- Badges are glass pills with readable text.
- Charts should be light, floating, and gently layered.

## Motion Rules

- Use soft lift, blur, opacity, and depth transitions.
- Avoid aggressive flashes, glitch effects, and heavy bounces.

## Output Checklist

- The page looks like a translucent modern OS surface.
- Glass never harms text readability.
- At least three visible layers have different depth.
